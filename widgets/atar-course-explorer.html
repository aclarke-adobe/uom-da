Graduate Degree Packages

If you already know the area you want to specialise in, or have a career in mind, a Graduate Degree Package can help secure a place in both an undergraduate and graduate course before you begin your studies.

Packages are available for both domestic and international students. The results shown may change based on your student type and the score you’ve selected in the ATAR slider above.

Please note:

  • Access Melbourne ATAR guarantees are also available for select Graduate Degree Packages for domestic students
  • For Bachelor of Fine Arts and Bachelor of Music packages, you'll need to submit supplementary creative tasks (such as a recorded audition or folio) as part of your undergraduate course application. See Auditions and interviews for full details.

Find out more about Graduate Degree Packages including benefits, general eligibility criteria and additional entry requirements.
